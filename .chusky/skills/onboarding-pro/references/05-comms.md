# Onboarding communications

## Principles

- Warm, clear, short
- Always include purpose + next step + timing
- One message, one primary ask
- Match company/owner voice from memory when available

## Templates (adapt, don’t sound robotic)

**Client kickoff confirm**
- Thanks + agenda + what to prepare + time/link

**Access request**
- Exact systems needed + who must click what + deadline

**First-win celebration**
- What shipped + impact + what happens next week

**New hire welcome**
- Excited + day-1 plan + who to ping + first goal

## Channels

- Prefer the channel the stakeholder already uses
- Meeting recaps within a few hours
- Reminders before deadlines, not only after misses
