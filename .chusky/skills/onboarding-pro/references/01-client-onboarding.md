# Client / customer onboarding

Goal: time-to-value. The client should achieve a first win fast and know what happens next.

## Phases

1. **Kickoff** — goals, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline
2. **Access & setup** — accounts, integrations, data, permissions
3. **Configuration** — product setup aligned to their use case
4. **First win** — one concrete outcome they care about
5. **Training** — who needs to know what; short sessions over dumps
6. **Handoff to success** — steady-state owner, support path, review date

## Kickoff packet

Capture:

- Primary contact and backup
- Business goal (one sentence)
- Success metric and target date
- Constraints (compliance, stack, blackout dates)
- Open questions

## Operating rules

- Own the sequence; do not wait to be chased
- After every meeting/call: written recap, owners, due dates
- Blockers become open loops with dueAt and nextAction
- Aria/Quinn context: commercial promises must match delivery plan
- No silent delay — if stuck >48h, escalate with options

## Done

Client hit the agreed first-win definition, knows how to get help, and a
follow-up review is scheduled.
