# Lead sourcing and contact enrichment

Clarify the user's offer, segment, region, company size, target role, requested count, budget, exclusions, and acceptable contact type. Reuse existing user data first. Discover companies in a bounded batch, deduplicate them, apply fit criteria, and enrich only the strongest matches. Verify company identity before matching a person.

Preserve exact provider status: verified/deliverable, catch-all, unknown/unverified, invalid, or no match. A successful API response or plausible email format is not verification. Keep company fit separate from contact availability. Return source and lookup date, role, fit reason, missing fields, exclusions, coverage, uncertainty, and actual spend. Return fewer results rather than padding. Never buy personal email or phone data or send outreach.
