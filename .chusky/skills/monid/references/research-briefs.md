# Decision briefs

**Companies:** verify identity and material claims against dated sources. Separate confirmed facts, provider estimates, and interpretation. Never invent private revenue, valuation, ownership, or intent. Give dated developments, useful angles, questions, unknowns, and spend; do not contact the company.

**Content:** ground page/FAQ/update recommendations in customer questions, competitor evidence, and demand/intent data. Keyword volume does not guarantee traffic. State first-hand evidence needed, rejected topics, and spend; do not publish or make unsupported health claims.

**Visibility:** compare identical domains, country, surfaces, dates, and settings. Keep citations distinct from mentions and recommendations. No historical baseline means no growth claim; citation counts do not prove what an individual chatbot will answer.

**Videos/creators:** deduplicate and report transcript coverage, source links, and timestamps. Captions do not prove visual inspection. Compare each creator to a stated recent sample and baseline; a snapshot does not prove growth or causation. Label explanations as hypotheses.

**Reviews:** balance ratings and dates, separate variants, remove duplicates, and include counterexamples. Anecdotes are not defect rates. Mark price unverified unless retrieved. Always retain scope, sample sizes, source dates, gaps, uncertainty, and actual spend.
