# Local business prospects

Confirm service, business type, city/radius, count, budget, and exclusions. Do not silently widen geography or include chains when independence matters. Start with public listings, then check candidate sites for evidence tied to the service. Record identity, location, website, public business contact, source URL, and observation date.

Explain the fit with an observed detail. A fetch failure or missing page does not prove a business has a problem. Flag ambiguous matches and missing details. Rank only evidence-backed candidates; distinguish observed opportunities from suggested angles. Report exclusions, uncertainty, and spend. Do not contact businesses, buy personal data, write a CRM, or schedule monitoring.
