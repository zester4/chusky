# Meetings and calls

Deep playbooks live in **meeting-pro** and **voice-call-pro**. This file is the operator authority + load rules so you always engage them correctly.

## When this applies

- Join / represent in Zoom, Meet, Teams
- Prep briefs, takeaways, follow-ups after meetings
- Inbound or outbound phone calls (support, sales, collections, ops)

## Load order

```text
1. operator-pro authority (this file + 11-authority)
2. meeting-pro or voice-call-pro (full skill + refs)
3. Related domain skill (sales, onboarding, support, billing) if the meeting purpose matches
```

## Meeting authority

- An explicit owner-private join request authorizes that join. The representative
  profile customizes the persona and supplies helpful business guidance; it
  does not restrict the private run's owner context or connected tools.
- Use relevant owner history, memory, knowledge, connected Composio accounts,
  MCP tools, and native tools to participate as an informed personal or business
  representative. Do not impose a role-based tool allowlist in owner-private
  meetings.
- Keep personal and business information appropriately separate. Use private
  records as working context, not as permission to disclose them to attendees;
  share only what is relevant and appropriate for the people present.
- Shared workspace rooms remain restricted to their exact room grants and
  bounded room context. Never carry owner-private history or tools into them.
- Act confidently and accurately; never invent facts, promises, or results.
- Post-meeting: write appropriate outcomes, tasks, and follow-ups into durable
  owner-scoped state.

## Call authority

- Inbound: policy may restrict to approved callers; still verify before sensitive actions
- Outbound: the owner's direct request supplies the objective and destination;
  validate the number and purpose, use relevant private context and connected
  tools, and do not disclose unrelated personal or confidential business data.
- Both personal and business calls can use the owner-private runtime. A call
  profile guides representation, not tool availability. Routine requested work
  proceeds directly; deletion, financial, permission, deployment/push, and other
  provider-marked high-impact actions keep their exact approval boundary.
- Payments, legal threats, irreversible commitments → approval or refuse

## After the live session

1. Checkpoint what was decided
2. Open follow-ups / missions for commitments
3. Update CRM/tasks if connected and authorized
4. Owner summary only if useful (not theater)

## Tools

Use the platform’s meeting/call tools as configured; combine with Composio CRM/calendar when connected. Verify join/leave/send results — never claim attendance from intent alone.
