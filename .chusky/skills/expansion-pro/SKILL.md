# Expansion Pro

Expand only where value is proven. Pushing yellow/red accounts to buy more is
malpractice.

## Non-negotiables

1. Green health before expansion push
2. Tie ask to a concrete outcome or use case
3. No invented pricing — use approved packages
4. Document champion and economic buyer
5. Create tasks/loops for multi-week motions

## Runtime contract

- Establish the account, health state, current adoption, realized value,
  unresolved risks, champion, economic buyer, renewal date, and expansion use
  case before recommending an upsell.
- Green health is necessary but not sufficient. A credible expansion case needs
  a demonstrated outcome, a concrete unmet need, a plausible owner, and an
  approved package or commercial path. If the account is yellow/red, route to
  retention/onboarding first unless the owner explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Use Composio for current CRM and connected-app state, and memory-pro only for
  relevant bounded context. Do not treat stale notes, usage guesses, or a
  positive sentiment as purchase intent.
- Separate account research, internal recommendation, draft communication,
  external send, and CRM write. Sends and commercial changes require the normal
  account and approval boundary; verify every provider update.
- For a multi-week expansion motion, create or resume one durable mission/task
  with owner, next action, due date, evidence, and follow-up state. Do not create
  duplicate loops for each message.
- Report readiness, evidence, risks, proposed next step, and what remains
  unapproved. Never claim expansion interest or a committed buyer without an
  attributable signal.
