# Handoffs

Handoffs must carry evidence and authority, not just a recommendation. Confirm
the receiving owner, source-of-truth record, requested action, and any approval
boundary before transferring the work.

- Commercial negotiation → Quinn / owner
- Implementation after yes → onboarding-pro / Aria
- Risk discovered mid-expand → retention-pro

Packet: account, thesis, proof, proposed package, constraints, next meeting goal.

Do not mark the expansion complete until the receiving system or owner has
acknowledged the packet and any external write has a provider receipt.
