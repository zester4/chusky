# Expansion comms

Use the Jev-selected communication route and the exact connected account. Keep
customer facts, proposed value, and the requested action distinct; a draft is
not a sent message and a sent message is not acceptance.

Lead with their goal → proof from current usage → proposal → clear ask.

Avoid feature dumps. One expansion thesis per thread.

Verify any usage, ROI, pricing, or outcome claim before sending. Log the
provider message receipt and next step when a message is authorized.
