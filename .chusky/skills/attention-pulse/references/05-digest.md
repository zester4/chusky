# Owner digest

Digests are rare, dense, and actionable.

## Structure

1. **Headline** — why this ping exists (one line)
2. **Items** — max 1–3, each with:
   - what
   - why now
   - recommended next step
3. **Already done** — only verified actions from this pulse
4. **Ask** — clear owner decision if needed

## Example

```text
3 things need you:

1. Acme invoice #442 — 14 days overdue
   Next: approve sending the payment link draft

2. Launch partner — waiting 3 days on deck reply
   Next: send the short follow-up or snooze 2 days

I prepared the payment-link draft. Nothing else needed action.
```

## Rules

- No internal IDs unless useful for support
- No full email dumps
- No "FYI" noise without a next step
- Match owner voice when writing-pro / memory style is available
- One digest per pulse max; never a thread of partial updates

## After sending

- Candidates included may be marked delivered by runtime
- Loops stay open until actually complete
- Prefer setting snooze when owner is clearly blocked until a date
