# Treg research workflows

## Company and people research

Define the target segment, geography, role, company size, count, exclusions,
and required fields. Discover companies first and deduplicate them. Confirm a
company's identity against its domain before enriching a person. Enrich only
the strongest matches. Treat provider match scores as provider-specific and
keep source URLs and lookup dates for every row.

An email is verified only when the result explicitly reports a verification
state. Keep verified/deliverable, catch-all, unknown, invalid, and no-match
separate. Verification does not authorize outreach.

## Signals and qualification

Hiring, technology adoption, content activity, and public business changes are
dated signals. They can support qualification or timing, but they do not prove
budget, intent, authority, or a purchase. State the observed fact, source,
date, and possible interpretation separately.

## Evidence briefs

For a company or partnership brief, verify material claims against original or
official sources, separate confirmed facts from provider estimates, and list
unknowns and questions. For local prospects, define the service and radius,
record observable evidence, and never infer a problem from a failed page load.
For SEO, ads, social, creators, videos, or reviews, preserve platform, sample,
date, units, links, timestamps, and completeness. A snapshot is not a trend;
an anecdote is not a rate; a caption is not visual proof.

Return a source-linked shortlist or brief with exclusions, coverage, actual
spend, and uncertainty. Do not pad a requested count, publish content, update
a CRM, send outreach, or schedule monitoring as a side effect of research.
