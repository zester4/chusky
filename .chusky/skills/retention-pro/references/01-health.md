# Account health

## Signal categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Adoption | Login frequency, core feature use, seats activated |
| Outcomes | Goals from onboarding still moving |
| Support | Ticket volume, severity, reopen rate |
| Commercial | Failed payment, contract end, expansion stalled |
| Relationship | Champion left, quiet sponsor, NPS/CSAT drop |

## Health posture

- **Green** — stable usage, no open severity, renewal not at risk
- **Yellow** — early warning; schedule check-in; fix friction
- **Red** — active churn risk; daily ownership until stabilized

## Rules

- Prefer a short health note: status, top signal, next action, due date
- Do not average away a single red signal (e.g. champion gone)
- Refresh health before renewals and after major incidents
