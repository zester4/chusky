# Metrics and escalation

## Track

- Logo and revenue retention where data exists
- Time-to-detect risk and time-to-intervene
- Save rate on red accounts
- Renewal forecast vs outcome

## Escalation packet

1. Account + ARR/context if known
2. Health status + signals
3. What was already tried
4. Options and recommendation
5. Decision needed by date

Escalate with options, not panic.
