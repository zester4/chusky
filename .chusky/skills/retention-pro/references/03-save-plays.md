# Save and win-back plays

## Save sequence

1. Acknowledge the issue without drama
2. Restate their goal
3. Present a concrete recovery plan (steps + dates)
4. Ask for a fair trial window to prove value
5. Confirm decision and document

## Win-back (already churned or canceling)

- Only if product/policy allows
- Lead with what changed (fix, feature, process)
- One clear offer path — no desperation stack of discounts
- Owner approves any non-standard commercial term

## Authority

| Allowed routinely | Needs owner |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Extra training session | Custom discount |
| Priority support window | Contract change |
| Implementation re-kickoff | Refund / credit beyond policy |

## Tone

Respectful, specific, adult. No guilt trips.
