# Call brief (mission packet)

Prepare before outbound or sensitive inbound handling:

1. **Who** — counterparty name, number, company
2. **Why** — objective in one sentence
3. **Useful facts** — relevant owner history, personal or business facts, and
   connected-app evidence appropriate to this call; verify before relying on it
4. **Disclosure limits** — unrelated personal memory, confidential business
   information in a personal call, unrelated accounts, and all credentials
5. **Authority** — what may be promised
6. **Script beats** — open → core → close
7. **Exit** — when to escalate or end
8. **After** — outcome fields to write

Mirror meeting-mission discipline: informed, not oversharing.
