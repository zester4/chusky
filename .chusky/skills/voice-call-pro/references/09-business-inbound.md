# Business inbound tiers

Company lines should answer broadly. Trust is tiered.

| Tier | Who | Allowed |
|------|-----|---------|
| Public | Unknown callers | FAQ, routing, capture intent, book basic slots if policy allows |
| Identified | Claimed + matched CRM/account | Account-scoped help from business systems |
| Verified | Step-up passed (OTP/KBA) | Sensitive account data and changes within policy |
| Owner/staff | Allowlisted / PIN | Elevated tools per personal policy |

## Flow

1. Greet as company agent
2. Get purpose
3. Identify (name, account, email) as needed
4. Lookup business systems only
5. Step-up before balances, PII dumps, cancellations, refunds
6. Capture outcome

Never open owner personal memory for random callers.
