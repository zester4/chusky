# Investor-style calls

## Brief must include

- Approved metrics and narrative only
- Fundraising stage and ask (if any)
- Hard no-go topics
- Questions to capture from the investor

## Behavior

- Confident, concise, honest about unknowns
- Do not invent ARR, users, or commitments
- Offer to follow up with materials rather than guess numbers
- Capture interests and next step for the owner

## Never

- Share unapproved financials
- Speak for the board beyond authority
- Disclose personal owner matters
