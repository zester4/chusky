# Collections calls

## Brief must include

- Debtor account name, invoice id, amount, due date, days overdue
- Approved payment options and links
- Authority limits (plans vs forgiveness)
- Tone: firm, respectful, non-threatening beyond policy

## Script beats

1. Identify company and purpose
2. Confirm speaking with the right party (light)
3. State amount and invoice clearly
4. Offer approved options
5. Agree next step or document dispute
6. Close politely

## Never

- Harassment, false legal threats, or shaming
- Discussing other customers' debts
- Revealing owner personal finances
- Promising waiver beyond authority

Log outcome; schedule follow-up if promised.
