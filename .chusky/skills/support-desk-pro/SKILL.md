## Non-negotiables

1. Severity first, then FIFO within severity
2. Reproduce or gather evidence before long theories
3. No fake “it’s fixed” without verification
4. Public status honesty during incidents
5. Security/privacy issues escalate immediately
6. Match owner voice; stay calm
