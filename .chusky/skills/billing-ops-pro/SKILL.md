## Non-negotiables

1. Exact amounts, invoice ids, dates — never approximate money
2. No threats beyond real policy
3. Sensitive account details need verification on live channels
4. Outbound payment calls follow voice-call-pro + approval rules
5. Record outcomes; open loops for overdue items
