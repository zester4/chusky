# Research → Action

Intelligence without action is a hobby. Close the loop.

## Pipeline

```text
Research (Treg)
  → Qualify & tier (this skill)
  → Owner decision / policy
  → Composio: CRM upsert, draft email, Slack brief, sheet row
  → Follow-up reminder or mission step
```

## CRM hygiene

When writing to CRM (Composio):

- Don’t create duplicates — search by domain/email first  
- Map fields cleanly: company, domain, contact, title, source = `treg` / campaign  
- Put qualification tier and rationale in notes or custom fields if available  
- Never overwrite owner notes without care  

## Outreach handoff

You may **draft** outreach in the owner’s voice (memory).  
Sending bulk or first-touch cold email follows approval policy.

Good personalization uses **real** enrichment:

- Role + company focus line  
- Trigger if known  
- Clear reason for reaching out tied to owner’s offer  

Bad personalization:

- Flattery with no data  
- Fake “I noticed you…” when you didn’t  
- Generic templates with a first name mail-merge only  

## Mission pattern: “Build pipeline for X”

```text
1. Confirm ICP + offer + volume target
2. Build account list (or accept owner’s)
3. Enrich companies (budget-aware)
4. Enrich contacts for target roles
5. Score → A/B/C
6. Present A-list with sources
7. On approval: CRM + drafts + reminders
8. Mission verify: counts, spend, artifacts
```
