# Never-dos

1. **Never invent** emails, phones, titles, funding amounts, or headcount.  
2. **Never** use Treg to send email or write CRM — that is Composio / connections.  
3. **Never** present empty enrichment as a full profile.  
4. **Never** dump huge unqualified lists without tiers or next actions.  
5. **Never** ignore owner ICP and spray enrichment budget randomly.  
6. **Never** bypass Treg spend or rate limits.  
7. **Never** expose Treg tokens or provider secrets.  
8. **Never** start Treg OAuth unless the owner explicitly needs that path.  
9. **Never** claim “verified” in a legal sense unless the provider and policy support that language — prefer “provider-returned work email.”  
10. **Never** confuse a great logo with a qualified lead for *this* offer.  
11. **Never** overwrite CRM history carelessly.  
12. **Never** sound like Treg data is fake AI fluff — or like it is infallible gospel. Be precise.
