# Lead Qualification

## Purpose

Qualification decides **who deserves time and spend** — not who looks interesting
on a list. Bad qualification wastes enrichment budget and burns the owner’s brand.

## Define “qualified” for this owner

Before scoring a batch, lock:

1. **ICP** — industry, size, geo, tech, use case  
2. **Disqualifiers** — competitors, students, wrong geo, no budget signal, etc.  
3. **Buying roles** — economic buyer, champion, user, blocker  
4. **Motion** — inbound, outbound, partner, expansion  

If memory already holds ICP, use it. If not, ask once, then save.

## Classic lenses (use what fits)

### BANT (classic)

| Letter | Question |
|--------|----------|
| Budget | Can they fund this class of solution? |
| Authority | Can this contact influence or decide? |
| Need | Is there a clear problem you solve? |
| Timing | Is there a reason to act in a defined window? |

### MEDDIC / MEDDPICC (complex B2B)

Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process,
Identify pain, Champion, Competition. Use when deals are multi-stakeholder.

### CHAMP (practical outbound)

Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization.

**Do not** force every letter when data is thin. Prefer honest partial scores
over fake completeness.

## Qualification outcomes

| Tier | Meaning | Default next step |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **A** | Strong ICP + role + plausible need | Prioritize; personalize outreach or meeting |
| **B** | Fit with gaps (role or timing unclear) | Light nurture or one clarifying touch |
| **C** | Weak fit or missing critical data | Deprioritize; don’t burn sequence slots |
| **DQ** | Hard disqualifier | Exclude; note reason |

## Inbound vs outbound

**Inbound:** Respect stated need; qualify speed-to-lead; don’t over-research
before a fast human response if policy wants rapid reply.

**Outbound:** Higher bar on ICP and role before multi-touch sequences.

## Output: qualification card

For each lead or account:

```text
Tier: A | B | C | DQ
Fit reasons: (3 bullets max, concrete)
Risks / gaps: (missing budget signal, wrong title, etc.)
Recommended motion: call | email | LinkedIn | nurture | ignore
Data sources: (Treg endpoints / CRM fields used)
```

## Conversational qualification

When talking to the owner:

- Recommend tiers with **reasons**, not vibes  
- Separate “good logo” from “good fit for *our* offer”  
- Call out when enrichment is strong but **need** is still unknown  

Unknown need is not automatic DQ — it is a research or discovery gap.
