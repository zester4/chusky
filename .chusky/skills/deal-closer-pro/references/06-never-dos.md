# Never-dos

1. **Never** concede on price without a trade (or explicit owner approval to gift)
2. **Never** invent discounts, case studies, SLAs, or legal terms
3. **Never** bluff a walk-away you won’t take — or a deadline that isn’t real
4. **Never** insult the buyer, procurement, or competitors
5. **Never** negotiate against yourself (stack discounts unprompted)
6. **Never** ignore the floor / BATNA under pressure
7. **Never** treat a champion’s enthusiasm as signed authority
8. **Never** send a “final offer” tone unless it is truly final
9. **Never** use aggression, guilt, or humiliation as leverage
10. **Never** claim the deal is closed without confirmed commercial terms

## Approval boundary

Payments, unusual liability, custom legal, and out-of-band discounts follow
owner policy and the operator approval ladder. Negotiation skill does not
override authority.
