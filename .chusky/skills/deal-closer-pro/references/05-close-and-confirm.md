# Close and confirm

## Closing styles (pick fit)

| Style | When |
|-------|------|
| **Choice close** | Two good options (e.g. annual vs monthly; phase 1 vs full) |
| **Summary close** | Recap interests met + package; ask for confirmation |
| **Next-step close** | Agreement on commercial shape → concrete path (send order form, legal redline deadline) |
| **Pilot / POV close** | Risk high; define success metrics and conversion terms up front |

Avoid manipulative forced closes. Clarity and reduced friction close more deals
than pressure.

## After verbal yes

1. Summarize **price, scope, term, payment, start date, open legal points**
2. Assign owners and dates for paperwork
3. Send written confirmation same day when possible
4. Open follow-ups for remaining items (use operator follow-up discipline)
5. Do not re-trade closed points without new information

## No-deal outcomes

A clean no beats a toxic yes. If below reservation:

- Thank them
- Leave the door open with specific conditions to reopen
- Log learnings; protect reputation

## Metrics of a good close

- Economics at or above floor
- Scope deliverable without heroics
- Relationship intact for renewal/expansion
- Written alignment, not fog
