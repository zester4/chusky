# Fashion and beauty

Each prompt is an original direction. Preserve the production pattern while changing the identity, brand, wardrobe, and wording.

1. Create a 9:16, 8-second luxury fragrance film: an original adult model walks through a pale limestone gallery, pauses, turns toward a narrow sunbeam, and raises an unbranded glass bottle. Begin with a slow tracking wide shot, push to a close-up of the bottle catching light, then rack focus to the eyes. Warm ivory, amber, and charcoal palette, quiet footsteps and one soft breath, restrained editorial grade, no readable label; add the exact campaign copy in post.
2. Create a 4:5, 10-second beauty clip of an original adult woman applying an unbranded serum in a quiet mineral bathroom. Start on a macro droplet sliding down the pipette, tilt to her cheek, then make a gentle half-orbit as she looks into a mirror. Keep natural pores and consistent hand anatomy, diffused north-window light, clean water and glass sound, premium magazine realism, no logos or text.
3. Create a 16:9, 12-second menswear lookbook shot in a brutalist courtyard. An original adult model walks from shadow into a rectangle of late-afternoon light; the camera tracks backward at chest height, then cuts to fabric texture and a final full-body still pose. Maintain the same taupe wool coat and brown leather shoes throughout, subtle film grain, wind moving only the coat hem, no brand marks.
4. Create a 9:16, 6-second jewelry editorial: an original adult hand places a sculptural unbranded ring on a stone pedestal, then rotates it once under a narrow spotlight. Use a macro slider move, shallow depth of field, black background, crisp metal reflections, slow deliberate motion, quiet room tone, no extra fingers, no text rendered in the video.
5. Create a 4:5, 15-second resort fashion vignette on an empty Mediterranean terrace. An original adult model in a rust linen outfit crosses the frame, pauses beside a linen curtain, and turns into the wind. Use one continuous lateral dolly, sun-bleached colors, fabric motion as the hero, distant sea ambience, consistent face and wardrobe, no recognizable architecture or logos.
6. Create a 16:9, 8-second editorial footwear film. A pair of fictional leather boots steps across wet black stone; start at ground level, follow the stride in a smooth tracking shot, then end on a close reflection in a puddle. Keep the boots identical, show believable weight and water displacement, cool blue light with one warm practical, restrained percussion, no label or watermark.
7. Create a 9:16, 10-second avant-garde beauty portrait. An original adult model holds a translucent sculptural veil near the face while colored light slowly shifts from cobalt to coral. Begin static and symmetrical, then make a slow push-in as the veil moves once. Keep facial identity stable, skin realistic, no melting features, sparse tonal sound design, no text.
8. Create a 4:5, 12-second independent fashion campaign in a sunlit parking structure. An original adult model descends one ramp in a monochrome green suit while the camera cranes gently from behind to profile. Use controlled motion blur only on the background, preserve garment seams and proportions, distant city ambience, graphic but original art direction, no known brand references.
9. Create a 16:9, 8-second couture detail film. Start on a needle passing through fictional hand-stitched fabric, pull back to reveal an artisan fastening a coat on an original adult model, then finish with the model taking one measured step. Warm workshop light, tactile wool and thread, quiet machine hum, consistent garment construction, no copied signature designs or logos.
10. Create a 9:16, 15-second premium skincare campaign with three beats: water beads on a ceramic dish, an original adult model turns toward soft daylight, and she presses a small unbranded cream jar closed. Use matching ivory and sage palette, gentle macro-to-medium transitions, realistic skin texture, clean water sounds, no claims or on-screen copy; reserve typography for post.
