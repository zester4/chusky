# Action, motion, and specialty production

These references were added from the user’s supplied examples and adapted as
original production patterns. They teach the agent to control action beats,
camera choreography, sound, transitions, and physical continuity. They are not
recipes for copying a film, game, brand, or recognizable character.

1. **Nordic snow pursuit:** Create a realistic cinematic sequence on a snowy Scandinavian night. Shot 1 is a low close tracking shot from behind of an original adult man running through deep snow. Shot 2 tracks from the front as an original wolf follows at a believable distance. The runner trips and rolls; Shot 3 widens as the wolf lunges but stops short. Shot 4 follows a fictional border-collie-like rescue dog entering from the side and diverting the wolf. Shot 5 is a close, non-graphic struggle; the wolf breaks away and howls. Shot 6 widens as several wolves approach without attacking. Shot 7 holds on the dog facing camera while the owner sits up behind it. Use tense evolving music, snow impacts, breath, and restrained animal behavior; maintain consistent weather, wardrobe, animal markings, and geography.

2. **Original cyberpunk action game trailer:** Create a dark game-CGI scene in a decayed neon city corner. An original masked assassin with a fictional glowing energy blade is surrounded by enemies, performs fluid non-graphic defensive movements, and uses brief teleport-like displacement effects between attacks. Start medium, rapidly pull back to reveal the circle of opponents, then track smoothly through the action. End with the assassin looking toward the camera. Use readable silhouettes, motivated camera movement, controlled motion blur, rain reflections, and original enemy designs; no recognizable franchise weapons, characters, logos, or copied game art direction.

3. **Shibuya-inspired athletic long take:** Create one continuous 9:16 cinematic shot in a fictional neon Japanese crosswalk district, not a recreation of a specific location. Begin with daytime street noise, arcade-like 8-bit accents, distant Japanese chatter, and shamisen-inspired strings. A fictional adult athlete in an original school-inspired uniform enters at speed, slows on the second beat, side-rolls close to the lens, vaults a low wall, and continues through the crowd. The camera stays low, lifts during the jump, rotates half a turn during the roll, corrects its horizon, follows a railing leap, and lands with the athlete on a lower platform. Keep one identity, outfit, and geography throughout; let the music cut briefly during weightlessness, then resume on the landing.

4. **Luxury perfume transformation:** Create an ultra-luxury perfume commercial for a fictional fragrance. Begin with a macro shot of a transparent rectangular bottle surrounded by swirling violet liquid, bubbles, and crisp water sounds. Dissolve to liquid ripples, then reveal original iris-like flowers underwater. Cut to the bottle tilted on a textured pale surface with refracted halos. Hold a sharp side still life on an invented label, then tilt upward as the bottle rotates slowly. Transition to an original adult Latina model in a pure white studio lifting the bottle near her neck and shoulder while looking calmly toward camera. Use dreamy electronic music with steady drums, consistent bottle geometry, no real brand, and add exact label typography in post if needed.

5. **Miniature kitchen macro film:** On a pure black background, use dramatic top light to reveal clean adult hands arranging a miniature cutting board and tiny knife. With solemn elegant classical music, use macro shots: tweezers place a garlic clove, the mini knife slices with amplified taps; a tiny pan sits over a safe warm light source, receiving one oil drop; a small egg cooks and is nudged with a spatula; finally, a miniature egg and mushroom are plated on a coin with a scallion garnish. Make scale, heat, hand anatomy, and food physics believable. End on a clean hold for the post-produced slogan: “Tiny Kitchen. Create your masterpiece. In miniature.”

6. **Original dark-fantasy combat trailer:** Create a vertical 9:16, high-energy non-graphic fantasy combat scene before a massive ancient stone sanctum. Two original heroes fight shoulder-to-shoulder against fictional shadow cultists: a heavily armored captain uses broad defensive sword arcs while an agile rogue moves between opponents with two glowing steel daggers. Use dynamic tracking, chiaroscuro, volumetric violet smoke, clear silhouettes, impact beats, and controlled motion blur. Keep the heroes’ faces, armor, weapons, and positions consistent; no gore, real insignia, famous characters, or copied game designs.

7. **Warm-to-cool uninterrupted journey:** Create a single Steadicam long take with no cuts. An original adult man leaves a warm yellow-lit fictional café, pushes through the door, enters a cool busy street, crosses between slow traffic with safe spacing, and descends into a dim subway staircase. Cars create controlled light trails without obscuring him. Transition lighting continuously from warm interior tungsten to cool daylight to fluorescent subway light. Preserve the man’s wardrobe, screen direction, and walking pace; use café ambience, traffic, footsteps, and subway hum as the evolving sound bed.
