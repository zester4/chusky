# UGC and social ads

Use a clear first-second hook, one idea per clip, natural delivery, platform-safe framing, and captions added in post when wording must be exact.

1. Create a 9:16, 15-second creator-style product testimonial. An original adult creator in a bright apartment opens with direct eye contact and says, “I changed one small part of my morning routine,” then demonstrates a fictional unbranded planner in three quick cuts. Handheld phone realism, natural pauses, exact dialogue only if audio is supported, leave top and bottom safe zones.
2. Create a 9:16, 10-second problem-solution ad for a fictional stain remover. Start with a close-up of a coffee mark and a surprised reaction, cut to one spray and wipe, then show the clean fabric in daylight. Keep the product label blank, use quick but believable action, creator voice and room tone, no unsupported performance claim.
3. Create a 9:16, 15-second street-style fashion clip. An original creator steps into frame wearing a fictional layered outfit, points to two garment details, turns once, and exits. Use a single vertical phone camera with one natural reposition, authentic city ambience, stable clothing identity, no copied influencer or logo.
4. Create a 9:16, 8-second food reaction clip. An original adult takes one bite of a fictional snack, pauses, smiles, and shows the package beside the plate. Bright kitchen light, imperfect handheld framing, one natural spoken reaction, unbranded pack, no exaggerated transformation or medical claim.
5. Create a 9:16, 15-second app explainer with an original presenter seated at a desk. The presenter states one benefit, gestures toward a blank phone screen, and finishes with a clear call to action. Keep the screen free of generated UI text; add the exact interface and captions in post, friendly daylight, clean jump cuts.
6. Create a 9:16, 10-second before-and-after home organization clip. Begin with a cluttered fictional desk, use a fast match cut to the same desk organized with neutral containers, then show one labeled-but-unreadable drawer opening. Preserve camera position, believable object continuity, upbeat room sound, no claim that the product solves every problem.
7. Create a 9:16, 15-second creator tutorial for a fictional camera accessory. The presenter holds the accessory at chest height, demonstrates one attachment in close-up, then shows the resulting stable shot. Use three clearly separated beats, consistent hands, practical indoor light, concise narration, leave space for post captions.
8. Create a 9:16, 8-second event teaser. A creator walks through an original night market, turns to camera, and reveals a glowing fictional event sign with no readable text. Use a quick reveal, neon reflections, lively ambient sound, motion that remains legible on a phone, title card added later.
9. Create a 9:16, 12-second wellness routine clip with an original adult instructor. Start with a calm inhale, demonstrate one safe stretch from a side angle, and end with a relaxed smile. Soft window light, tripod realism, no medical promises, simple ambient sound, captions and branding reserved for post.
10. Create a 9:16, 15-second customer-story ad. An original adult speaks in a quiet workspace about a fictional service using three short sentences, with cutaways to hands completing a generic task. Natural eye movement, believable pauses, clear audio, no personal data on screens, no invented testimonial metrics.
