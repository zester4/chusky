# Product advertising

These directions emphasize product geometry, controlled motion, legible visual hierarchy, and post-production-safe copy.

1. Create a 16:9, 8-second hero film for a fictional matte-black travel mug. On a dark stone table, condensation forms, the lid rotates open, and hot steam rises as a sun stripe crosses the logo-free surface. Use a slow 180-degree product orbit, one macro insert, realistic reflections, low warm key light, subtle ceramic clink, no invented text.
2. Create a 9:16, 10-second performance ad for a fictional running shoe. Start with the shoe suspended over a wet track, drop it into a runner's stride, follow one footfall in slow motion, and end on a clean side profile. Keep sole geometry and laces consistent, show believable water splash, crisp sunrise light, energetic but uncluttered sound bed, leave safe margins for captions.
3. Create a 4:5, 8-second food product film for an original citrus soda in a plain glass bottle. Ice drops into a tumbler, the bottle pours a sparkling stream, bubbles rise, and a lime peel lands beside it. Use a locked-off tabletop with two macro push-ins, cream and lime palette, convincing condensation and carbonation, no real brand or readable label.
4. Create a 16:9, 12-second fictional smart-speaker launch film. A small unbranded device sits in a calm home; a hand places it down, a soft light ring responds, and the room transitions from morning to evening. Use a measured dolly-in and one overhead cut, show no private data or interface text, warm natural sound, final product hero on negative space.
5. Create a 9:16, 6-second cosmetics ad for a fictional coral lip color. A clean unbranded tube opens, a creamy stroke spreads across a glass surface, and the tube returns upright. Use macro photography, smooth controlled rotation, saturated coral against pale pink, accurate texture and highlights, no human face, no copied packaging.
6. Create a 16:9, 15-second furniture spot for a fictional modular chair. In an empty sunlit room, separate cushions slide into a finished chair as if guided by invisible rails; an adult sits once and relaxes. Use a static architectural frame plus one slow lateral move, preserve material seams, soft room tone, no impossible collisions, no text.
7. Create a 4:5, 8-second watch advertisement for a fictional stainless-steel chronograph. Begin with a wrist in warm sunlight, rack focus from the knuckles to the dial, then rotate the wrist once to reveal the bracelet. Keep the dial markings stable and minimal, 85mm shallow focus, realistic reflections, no brand name, no extra sub-dials appearing.
8. Create a 9:16, 10-second eco-cleaning product ad. An unbranded refill pouch stands beside a reusable bottle; a measured stream fills the bottle, a cloth wipes a countertop, and the scene ends on the two objects beside a small plant. Bright natural kitchen light, crisp water motion, optimistic sound, no unverifiable sustainability claims in the generated frame.
9. Create a 16:9, 8-second premium headphones film. Floating fictional headphones rotate slowly in a black studio while thin waves of light suggest sound, then the ear cushions compress gently under a hand. Keep the product symmetrical and physically plausible, controlled rim light, deep bass pulse, no UI or logo, ample negative space for post copy.
10. Create a 4:5, 12-second specialty coffee advertisement. Fresh beans fall into a grinder, grounds bloom in a glass brewer, and a cup is set on a wooden counter. Use three match-cut close-ups with consistent warm amber grade, authentic steam and liquid physics, gentle café ambience, fictional packaging with no readable text.
