# Avatar and presenter videos

These prompts are for explainers, training, sales, and creator-led content. Use an original presenter or an authorized avatar only.

1. Create a 16:9, 30-second presenter video with an original professional adult standing in a bright studio. Deliver three concise points about a fictional scheduling product, with natural hand gestures, direct eye contact, clean pauses between points, and a neutral background with room for captions. Do not invent UI text.
2. Create a 9:16, 20-second creator-style presenter clip. An original adult sits at a desk, opens with one question, demonstrates a fictional notebook, and closes with one call to action. Use friendly conversational delivery, small natural movements, phone-safe framing, and no real person likeness.
3. Create a 16:9, 45-second employee onboarding lesson. An original presenter explains a fictional safety checklist in four labeled beats, pausing after each beat for captions. Medium shot, calm delivery, clear pronunciation, neutral office background, exact labels added in post.
4. Create a 16:9, 30-second sales explainer. An original presenter introduces a fictional analytics service, gestures toward three blank graphic panels, and summarizes one measurable workflow benefit without making unsupported numerical claims. Keep the camera locked and audio clean.
5. Create a 9:16, 15-second FAQ response. An original presenter answers “How does setup work?” in two short sentences, then points to a blank phone mockup. Natural expression, quick social pacing, no generated interface copy, captions reserved for post.
6. Create a 16:9, 40-second multilingual training variant using the same original presenter, wardrobe, camera, and gestures. Speak the supplied approved script clearly in the requested language, with clean pauses and no improvisation. Keep lip sync and timing consistent; confirm live audio support first.
7. Create a 9:16, 20-second testimonial-style fictional case study with an original authorized actor. The speaker describes a workflow improvement without claiming to be a real customer, uses one supporting cutaway to a blank dashboard, and ends naturally. No fabricated company names, metrics, or endorsements.
8. Create a 16:9, 30-second internal announcement. An original presenter speaks warmly to a team, delivers a supplied three-sentence script exactly, and ends on a calm hold for the title card. Use a simple branded-color background but no generated logo or text.
9. Create a 9:16, 15-second product education clip. An original presenter holds a fictional unbranded device, names three physical features from the supplied script, and rotates it once. Keep hands and product geometry stable, use even studio light, and add labels in post.
10. Create a 16:9, 60-second course introduction. An original instructor welcomes viewers, previews three lessons with deliberate pauses, and points toward empty on-screen spaces for chapter titles. Stable medium framing, warm educational tone, accurate mouth movement, no copyrighted set or personality imitation.
