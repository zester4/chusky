# Advertising and Product-Campaign Prompts

Use these for paid-social creative, ecommerce, product launches, and campaign concepts. Define the audience and conversion purpose, then make the product the unambiguous visual hero.

1. `Create a premium 4:5 paid-social ad for a matte-black reusable water bottle aimed at urban professionals. Bottle centered on a limestone pedestal, condensation and subtle green leaf, warm directional studio light, clean negative space on the left for copy, realistic commercial photography, preserve cap and logo geometry, no readable text, no extra bottles.`
2. `Create a 1:1 launch ad for a plant-based protein bar called FIELD FUEL. Show the exact wrapper design from the reference beside oats, cacao, and a sliced banana on a pale stone surface. Bright natural daylight, energetic but premium palette, product label facing camera, preserve all package text and proportions, no health claims, no invented badges.`
3. `Create a 9:16 mobile ad for a financial-planning app. Show a calm professional checking a phone in a bright home office, subtle abstract upward path in the background, blue and mint palette, leave the upper third empty for headline copy, authentic lifestyle photography, no readable screen data, no money piles, no fake UI text.`
4. `Create a 4:5 luxury fragrance advertisement for ORIEL. Place the exact bottle from the reference on black stone with a single ivory flower, dramatic side light, deep shadows, restrained ivory and gold palette, editorial perfume photography, preserve bottle proportions and label, no extra words, no liquid splashes.`
5. `Create three coordinated 1:1 ads for a meal-delivery service: fresh ingredients, prepared dish, and family dinner. Keep one consistent warm kitchen environment and coral/cream brand palette, food photorealistic and appetizing, clear hero composition in each frame, no visible brand text, no duplicate utensils, no distorted hands.`
6. `Create a 16:9 B2B campaign hero for workforce-management software. Show a diverse operations team reviewing a clean wall dashboard in a bright warehouse office, focus on collaboration and clarity, navy and teal accents, left-side negative space for copy, authentic documentary photography, no readable dashboard data, no logos, no staged handshake.`
7. `Create a 2:3 outdoor-ad concept for a fictional electric bicycle. Show the bike leaning against a coastal overlook at golden hour, rider helmet hanging from the handlebar, strong silhouette and visible frame details, cinematic but believable photography, generous sky for headline, preserve product geometry, no invented text.`
8. `Create a 4:5 beauty ad for a hydrating face serum. One glass dropper bottle on wet translucent acrylic with soft peach and lavender light, macro commercial photography, exact label facing forward, clean top margin for copy, refined water droplets, no claims, no additional bottles, no fake certification seal.`
9. `Create a fast 9:16 social ad concept for a local bookstore. Show a joyful reader carrying a stack of colorful books through a sunlit independent bookstore, energetic candid movement, warm red and mustard accents, clear lower-third negative space for offer copy, no readable book titles, no fake logos.`
10. `Create a premium 1:1 ad for a noise-cancelling headphone product. Headphones suspended against a soft graphite background with subtle concentric sound-wave light, crisp material and hinge detail, luxury tech photography, centered product, high contrast, no person, no extra text, no distorted earcups.`

## Advanced campaign references

11. `Create a classic vertical advertising poster for a fictional citrus soda. Center a chilled clear glass bottle filled with pale yellow-green soda against a light cream background, covered in believable condensation. Add the invented embossed brand “LIMETTA”, the oversized lime-green cursive word “Zesty” arcing above the bottle, a small hand-drawn lemon slice, an oval “ICE COLD” badge, and the exact ingredient line “SPARKLING WATER · CANE SUGAR · LIME JUICE · LEMON PEEL · NATURAL FLAVORS”. Use retro halftone print texture, lime, lemon, and cream palette, crisp legible typography, no real logos, no watermarks, and no extra text.`
12. `Create a real candid vertical lifestyle advertisement for a fictional watch brand. Use a low-angle over-the-shoulder view of an original bearded adult man in sunglasses and a white cotton shirt, his head and shoulder heavily out of focus in the foreground. His raised left arm crosses toward the sun; only the stainless-steel chronograph watch is sharp, with a sunray olive dial, three silver sub-dials, date window, baton hands, tachymeter bezel, and brushed-polished bracelet, with no brand name on the dial. Use a saturated cyan sky, two blurred palms, warm late-afternoon light, 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, true-to-life color, and subtle grain. Add only “HALDEN” and smaller “WATCHES” in clean white wide-tracked geometric caps; no other logos or text.`
13. `Create an original premium skincare campaign as an ultra-close editorial beauty portrait. Use a different Black adult female model with natural pores, luminous hydrated skin, subtle glossy lips, minimal makeup, calm confidence, and a three-quarter angle. Place one hand softly near the jaw and the other near the temple with a nude manicure, against a warm ivory studio background with soft beauty lighting. Overlay the exact white modern sans-serif word “LUMAIRE” across the mid-face and the smaller spaced line “SKIN, SIMPLY LUMINOUS.” No existing brands, famous likenesses, watermarks, plastic retouching, or extra text.`
