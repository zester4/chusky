# Fashion and Fashion-Brand Prompts

Use these for editorial campaigns, lookbooks, ecommerce, streetwear, luxury, beauty, and fashion social content. Preserve garment construction, fit, logos, and material details when editing a reference.

1. `Create a luxury 4:5 campaign image for a midnight-black silk evening gown. One model stands in a quiet brutalist gallery, three-quarter pose, full garment visible, negative space above the shoulders for a headline. Hard window light creates sculptural shadows, deep charcoal and silver palette, editorial fashion photography, 85mm lens, refined natural skin, exact seam and drape detail, no extra jewelry, no readable text, no distorted hands.`
2. `Create a 9:16 streetwear lookbook image for a fictional brand called NORTHLINE. Show a model in an oversized rust-orange technical jacket, wide black trousers, and white sneakers walking through a rain-wet concrete underpass. Low-angle full-body framing, motion in the background but sharp clothing, cool blue shadows with warm sodium highlights, premium campaign photography, preserve garment proportions, no fake brand text, no watermark.`
3. `Edit the referenced jacket photo into a clean ecommerce image. Preserve the exact color, zipper, stitching, pockets, logo placement, and silhouette. Place the jacket on an invisible model against a warm-white studio background, front-facing, even soft lighting, accurate fabric texture, centered with generous margins, no wrinkles that do not exist, no accessories, no added text.`
4. `Create a 1:1 beauty campaign for a glass skincare bottle on a pale travertine slab. Surround it with three translucent green leaves and tiny water droplets, macro product photography, soft sunrise backlight, cream and sage palette, premium minimal composition, label facing camera and completely legible, no invented claims, no extra bottles, no hands.`
5. `Create a cinematic 16:9 editorial fashion portrait of a person wearing a sculptural cobalt-blue coat on a windswept coastal cliff. Subject placed on the right third, storm clouds opening to a narrow beam of sunlight, textured wool and sharp tailoring, dramatic but authentic photography, desaturated ocean palette with cobalt focal color, no surreal anatomy, no logo, no text.`
6. `Create six coordinated 4:5 campaign frames for a sustainable footwear collection: recycled canvas sneakers in cream, forest, and clay. Maintain one consistent studio set, soft side light, tactile materials, clean catalog composition, each frame a distinct angle or styling combination, product silhouette accurate, no repeated shoes within a frame, no readable text.`
7. `Create a premium 3:4 jewelry editorial with a close-up hand wearing a thin brushed-gold ring and small emerald stone. Use a dark velvet background, controlled spotlight, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, elegant negative space, exact ring geometry, anatomically correct fingers, no extra jewelry, no watermark, no text.`
8. `Create a fashion magazine cover concept in 2:3 portrait format featuring a model in a white architectural suit against a vivid red seamless background. Leave the upper and left margins clean for masthead and cover lines, centered confident pose, crisp studio lighting, high-fashion editorial styling, no generated words or fake logos, preserve a clean cover-ready layout.`
9. `Edit the referenced handbag image into a campaign scene without changing the bag's shape, hardware, leather grain, or logo. Place it on a sunlit café table beside a folded newspaper with unreadable pages, 45-degree product angle, warm Mediterranean palette, realistic commercial photography, soft shadow, no hands, no extra bag, no invented text.`
10. `Create a fashion week backstage documentary photograph: a diverse designer adjusting the sleeve of a model in a silver pleated garment, garment rack and soft work lights in the background, candid mid-action framing, authentic texture and slight film grain, subject faces natural, clothing construction accurate, no celebrity likeness, no readable brand names.`

## Advanced campaign references

11. `Create an original high-impact fashion sale campaign poster using a gritty monochrome halftone portrait of an original adult female model, close chest-up crop, turned slightly sideways with one hand near her hair and face partly shadowed. Use a dark sleeveless outfit with no logos. Apply dense round black halftone dots across the entire image on a pale cool-grey base, crushed blacks, photocopy-zine imperfections, and fluorescent red graphic blocks. Add the fictional wordmark “ATELIER NOIR” above a central red block containing “MID-SEASON” and “UP TO 55%”; add a separate date block reading “03.14 — 03.23”. Use modern grotesk typography, subtle diagonal tape strips, asymmetrical Swiss-influenced hierarchy, generous negative space, no real brands, no celebrity likeness, no watermarks, and no gibberish text.`
12. `Create an editorial menswear photograph shot through a slightly dirty car window on a Parisian street. Show an original adult male model from the chest up in a heavy brown-and-ochre boucle tweed jacket with a matching cap, direct neutral gaze, warm brown, amber, cream, and white-car framing. Let the glass create the diffusion, reflections, grain, and documentary intimacy; use a 90mm perspective, lifted blacks, soft pastel midtones, and natural film texture. No celebrity resemblance, logos, or added typography.`
13. `Create a portrait 4:5 fashion campaign photograph with soft film grain and faded warm tones. An adult man moves quickly left to right past a weathered white plaster wall with a terracotta band; use a slow shutter so the loose ecru linen shirt and wide rust trousers become horizontal motion streaks while one brown suede loafer and the wall texture remain nearly sharp. Crop the head slightly at the top. Add only the exact small white text “the summer edit is here” with “summer” bold and “(ondrastudio.com)” beneath; no other text or logos.`
14. `Create an original luxury menswear campaign in a vertical frame against full-height rich walnut panels. Center an original adult male model from head to shoes in a taupe-brown high-collar wool outfit with relaxed trousers and dark leather shoes, holding a mushroom-brown structured leather weekender with no logo. Overlay one precise horizontal three-panel strip: jacket fabric and construction, a side-profile close-up of the same model, and a small walking full-body view carrying the same bag. Keep identity, outfit, lighting, proportions, and wood environment consistent across panels. Add only a small fictional cream script label “Maison Alder”; no famous brands, copied silhouettes, duplicated people, warped hands, or CGI-looking materials.`
15. `Create a high-end warm architectural fashion story with a different original male model and a monochromatic camel-and-walnut wardrobe. Use a quiet hotel corridor with vertical timber, soft directional afternoon light, and a large area of calm negative space. Produce three cohesive editorial frames: front portrait, detail of textured tailoring, and full-body walking shot. Preserve the same model and garment across frames, emphasize realistic wool, leather, and skin texture, and add no brand name or decorative text.`
