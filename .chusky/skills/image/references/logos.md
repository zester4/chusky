# Logo and Brand-Identity Prompts

Use these for brand marks, wordmarks, icons, identity exploration, and brand systems. For final vector delivery, inspect the live model catalog and choose a model that explicitly supports vector/SVG output; do not call raster output SVG.

1. `Create eight distinct logo-mark concepts for HARBOR, a workforce-management software company. Explore simple geometric symbols suggesting coordination, reliability, and people moving together. Navy, teal, and warm white palette, strong silhouette at favicon size, flat vector-like treatment, each concept isolated on a clean grid, no invented tagline, no mockup, no gradients.`
2. `Design a modern wordmark for NORTHSTAR HEALTH using the exact spelling “NORTHSTAR HEALTH”. Use a calm deep-blue sans-serif letterform with one subtle star detail integrated into the typography, high legibility at small sizes, horizontal lockup, white background, no extra words, no misspellings, no stock-symbol lookalikes.`
3. `Create a minimal app icon for a private meeting assistant named LUMA. Use one abstract speech shape intersecting a small light point, rounded geometry, cobalt and violet palette, centered in a square with generous padding, flat color, no letters, no shadows, no phone mockup.`
4. `Create a premium monogram logo for the initials “AR” for a boutique architecture studio. Interlock the two letters using precise thin black lines, balanced negative space, square composition, editorial Swiss design influence, white background, no extra lettering, no decorative frame.`
5. `Create a playful but professional emblem for a children’s science club called SPARK LAB. Combine a small atom orbit with a friendly geometric spark, bright yellow, electric blue, and charcoal, bold shapes that work in one color, centered isolated mark, no mascot face, no slogan, no tiny details.`
6. `Create a set of four coordinated brand symbols for a sustainable coffee company called FIELD & FINCH: primary mark, compact icon, bean pattern, and stamp emblem. Use an earthy olive, clay, and cream palette, hand-drawn botanical geometry, consistent stroke language, show each separately with clear spacing, exact name only where requested, no fake certification badges.`
7. `Create a sharp geometric logo for a cybersecurity company named VAULTLINE. Use a simple shield formed from two interlocking lines, black and electric green, high contrast, scalable silhouette, no padlock cliché, no gradients, no 3D effects, no text except the exact name “VAULTLINE”.`
8. `Create a refined wordmark and symbol for a luxury fragrance brand called ORIEL. Use elegant high-contrast serif lettering with a small abstract sun-disc symbol, black, ivory, and muted gold, generous tracking, centered presentation on ivory paper, no bottle mockup, no extra words, preserve exact spelling.`
9. `Create six icon concepts for a logistics dashboard: route, warehouse, shipment, alert, delivery, and verified. Use one consistent rounded-line system, dark navy and teal, clear at 24 pixels, identical stroke weight, presented on a simple 2-by-3 grid, no labels, no gradients, no irrelevant symbols.`
10. `Create a brand identity moodboard for an independent film studio called CINDER FRAME. Include a wordmark direction, abstract flame-frame symbol, monochrome palette with one burnt-orange accent, film grain texture, typography samples represented by blank shapes rather than generated text, and three application tiles. Keep it conceptual, coherent, and free of invented trademarks.`
