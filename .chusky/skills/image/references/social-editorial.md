# Social, Editorial, and Content Prompts

Use these for thumbnails, carousels, thought-leadership graphics, newsletters, and editorial storytelling. Keep a consistent visual system across variants.

1. `Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail about “How Teams Actually Collaborate”. Show two contrasting halves: chaotic scattered sticky notes on the left and a calm organized planning board on the right. Bold visual contrast, large empty title area across the top, navy, yellow, and coral palette, no generated words, no fake logos, no clutter.`
2. `Create a three-frame 1:1 carousel about remote-work rituals. Frame one: morning planning desk; frame two: focused video collaboration; frame three: end-of-day shutdown. Use the same warm cream, cobalt, and orange editorial illustration style, consistent character design, clear negative space for later captions, no readable text.`
3. `Create a 4:5 LinkedIn thought-leadership graphic about humane automation. Show a human hand guiding a small luminous tool among calm geometric workflow nodes, sophisticated navy and teal palette, editorial 3D illustration, centered focal point, generous margins, no robot cliché, no generated words.`
4. `Create a 9:16 Instagram story for a sustainable fashion drop. Show folded natural-fiber garments in clay, cream, and olive arranged as a tactile flat lay, direct overhead view, sunlight and soft shadows, clean top and bottom space for later copy, no visible labels, no extra accessories.`
5. `Create a 16:9 newsletter header about small-business resilience. Show a compact storefront glowing during a rainstorm with one warm interior light, cinematic editorial illustration, deep blue and amber palette, subject slightly right with left-side copy space, hopeful but not sentimental, no text, no logos.`
6. `Create a 1:1 social announcement for a new podcast about design leadership. Use a bold microphone silhouette intersecting a geometric sunrise, electric blue, tangerine, and black, modern flat poster style, crisp central composition, leave bottom quarter clean for episode details, no generated lettering.`
7. `Create a 4:5 editorial portrait illustration of a community organizer speaking with neighbors outside a city library. Diverse adults, candid gestures, warm late-afternoon light, ink-and-gouache texture, muted brick, sage, and cream palette, believable anatomy, no readable signs, no political slogans.`
8. `Create a 9:16 short-form video cover for a recipe series called “Five-Minute Lunch”. Show a bright bowl of grains, greens, and roasted vegetables from a close overhead angle, energetic yellow and green palette, clean upper space for the title, realistic food photography, no fake text, no extra utensils.`
9. `Create a 2:3 magazine-style editorial image about the future of cities. Show a tree-lined pedestrian street with trams, cyclists, and mixed-use buildings, subtle futuristic infrastructure but believable architecture, morning haze, muted blue and green palette, strong leading lines, no readable signs, no flying cars.`
10. `Create five cohesive 1:1 quote-card backgrounds for a leadership newsletter. Abstract paper, soft shadow, one small geometric accent in navy, coral, and cream, varied but compatible compositions, large clear center area for later typography, no generated quote text, no watermark, no border clipping.`
