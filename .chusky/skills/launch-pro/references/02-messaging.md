# Messaging and assets

Use verified product facts and the approved launch brief. Drafting copy or
creating an asset does not authorize publication, and external claims must be
traceable to a source or clearly labeled as a proposal.

- One core narrative; secondary points support it
- Claims must be true and approved
- Assets list: page, email, social, in-product, sales one-pager
- Version lock time: after lock, changes need explicit decision
- Leo/Maya for creative and channel formatting; verification before publish
- Keep the final asset/version ID and approval decision with the launch record.
